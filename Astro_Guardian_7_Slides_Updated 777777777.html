02 The Challenge

Long-duration space missions expose astronauts to radiation, isolation, low gravity, and other harsh conditions. These challenges can affect physical and mental health.

01Radiation

Exposure to space radiation during a mission.

02Isolation

Distance from Earth and limited contact with others.

03Low Gravity

Reduced gravity can affect how the body functions.

04Harsh Environment

Unusual conditions can place extra demands on the crew.

03 The Problem & Solution

Astronauts may experience changes in the immune system, bone density, heart function, and mental health. Medical teams on Earth cannot always monitor them in real time because of great distances and communication delays.

Immune System

Immune function may change during spaceflight.

Bone Density

Bone density may decrease over time.

Heart Function

Cardiovascular function may be affected.

Mental Health

Isolation and stress can affect well-being.

Our solution is Astro Guardian: a smart dashboard that combines astronaut health monitoring and radiation tracking in one system, identifies risk levels, and provides alerts and suggested actions.

Monitor

Bring health indicators and radiation exposure together.

Analyze

Assess indicators and classify the level of risk.

Alert

Show smart warnings and suggested next steps.

04 Key Features

Astro Guardian brings the main astronaut health and mission indicators into one clear dashboard.

01Vital Signs

Heart rate, blood pressure, blood oxygen, and body temperature.

02Radiation Tracking

Current exposure rate and cumulative radiation dose.

03Health Indicators

Immune index, estimated bone-density loss, and stress level.

04Smart Alerts

Warnings and suggested actions when risk increases.

05Crew Monitoring

A clear view of multiple crew members.

06CSV Export

Export mission data logs as a CSV file for later review.

05 How It Works & AI

The system follows five main steps to turn incoming readings into clear health information.

01Sensors

Collect health and radiation data.

02Data Processing

Receive and prepare the readings for analysis.

03AI Analysis

Analyze indicators and classify risk as Low, Medium, or High.

04Data Storage

Store readings for later review.

05Dashboard

Display health status, alerts, and recommendations clearly.

Random Forest

In the proposed system design, a Random Forest model classifies risk using a group of health and radiation indicators.

Risk Levels

The dashboard presents risk as Low, Medium, or High to make changes easier to recognize and respond to.

Prototype note: the current version uses Simulated Data. Training and validation with real spaceflight and medical data are future development steps.

      07 Technology, Impact & Future Development

      The interactive dashboard is built with web technologies, with a proposed architecture for the complete system.

      Frontend

      HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for the interactive dashboard.

      Backend

      Python and FastAPI for data processing in the full system design.

      Database

      PostgreSQL for storing readings and mission logs.

      AI Integration

      Scikit-learn and Random Forest for risk classification in the proposed design.

      Project Impact

      Help astronauts follow their health more clearly, support Mission Control in identifying risks earlier, and provide organized data for medical teams to review.

      Future Development

      Integrate wearable sensors, train the model using real spaceflight and medical data, and improve communication with medical teams on Earth.

      The current website is a browser-based prototype using simulated data. Backend, database, and machine-learning integration are part of the proposed full-system architecture.
